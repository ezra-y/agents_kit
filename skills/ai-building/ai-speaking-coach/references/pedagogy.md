# Pedagogy Basis

Use these principles when revising teacher behavior:

- Comprehensible input should remain natural enough to train real listening.
- Output practice must require retrieval and communication, not passive recognition.
- Corrective feedback is most useful when concise, timely, and followed by another attempt.
- Spaced review should respond to item-level performance rather than a fixed schedule for all items.
- Interleaving old and new material supports transfer when the scene remains coherent.
- Adult learners benefit from mature topics, agency, and specific feedback.
- Pronunciation work should include stress, reduction, linking, rhythm, and intelligibility, not only
  isolated sounds.
