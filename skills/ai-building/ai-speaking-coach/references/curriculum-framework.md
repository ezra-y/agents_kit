# Speaking Curriculum Framework

This framework defines what the reusable coach develops. It is a planning reference, not a
sentence bank, a fixed course sequence, or an official proficiency rating.

## Curriculum Outcome

Help the learner complete personally relevant spoken tasks with increasing independence,
intelligibility, naturalness, and ability to recover when communication breaks down.

## Capability Map

Evaluate progress through observable communication:

| Capability | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Task completion | Achieves the real purpose of the exchange |
| Listening | Understands the information needed to respond |
| Interaction | Takes turns, follows up, confirms, and repairs |
| Language resources | Retrieves sufficient vocabulary, chunks, grammar, and pragmatic choices |
| Fluency | Speaks in workable chunks without delays that block the task |
| Intelligibility | Uses pronunciation, stress, and rhythm that are easy enough to understand |

These capabilities may develop at different rates. Accent imitation is not the overall goal.

## Communication Modes

Plans should collect evidence from:

- Interpretive communication: understand spoken messages and relevant details.
- Interpersonal communication: exchange information and negotiate meaning.
- Presentational communication: describe, narrate, explain, or express a position.

## Target-Task Families

Choose only families that serve the learner's current life:

- Manage interaction: start, maintain, clarify, confirm, repair, and close.
- Build relationships: small talk, invitations, shared interests, and personal stories.
- Handle daily logistics: appointments, purchases, services, travel, and unexpected problems.
- Work or study with others: explain, ask, update, coordinate, discuss, and present.
- Express meaning precisely: compare, justify, soften, disagree, and respond with appropriate
  register.
- Understand connected speech: decode natural reductions, linking, stress, and conversational pace.

Specialized goals may add new task families without changing the capability map.

## Progression Evidence

Use a task-specific progression rather than a universal sentence order:

```text
supported completion
-> independent completion in a familiar task
-> independent completion with a meaningful variation
-> delayed retrieval and transfer to a related task
```

Progress requires spoken performance. Exposure, explanation, or correct imitation alone is not
mastery.

## Curriculum Layers

```text
curriculum framework
  -> defines capabilities and target-task families

learner plan
  -> selects the current outcome, tasks, bottleneck, and success evidence

teaching corpus
  -> supplies teachable expressions, patterns, examples, and media

finalized lesson
  -> combines current tasks, due retrieval, errors, and selected content
```

The framework is not indexed in LanceDB as teachable content. The current private corpus is
indexed, but retrieval does not decide curriculum priorities. A future reusable distribution may
split the corpus into installable content packs without changing this relationship.

## Basis

The structure follows the action-oriented
[CEFR Companion Volume](https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4),
the three communication modes in
[NCSSFL-ACTFL Can-Do Statements](https://www.actfl.org/educator-resources/ncssfl-actfl-can-do-statements),
and the multi-component account of speaking assessment reviewed by
[De Jong](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-linguistics-030521-052114).
